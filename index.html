Frequently asked questions

Is Cash BD free to use?+

Yes. Downloading and creating an account is free. You only deposit money if you want to post your own jobs.

How do I withdraw my earnings?+

Open the Wallet tab and request a payout to bKash or Nagad. Withdrawals need the minimum balance and the required number of active referrals, and a small service fee applies. All details are shown in the app before you confirm.

How much does one ad pay?+

Each ad view earns a small reward, and there is a daily limit. Larger earnings come from micro-tasks. The current rates are always shown in the app.

Why was my account warned or banned?+

Fake proofs, repeated rejected tasks and multiple accounts on one device break the rules. Submit genuine work only. If you think a mistake was made, contact support on Telegram.

The APK won't install. What should I do?+

Go to your phone Settings and allow “Install unknown apps” for the browser you used to download the file, then open the APK again.
